> A modern, responsive, and dynamic **Food Ordering Website** where users can explore delicious menus, check the gallery, read reviews, and order food online in just a few clicks.  

🌐 **Live Demo:** [Food Website](https://food-wesbite-oo.vercel.app/)  

---

## ✨ Features  

✅ **Modern UI/UX** – Fully responsive and user-friendly design.  
✅ **Home Page** – Catchy hero section with CTA: *Order Now*.  
✅ **About Section** – Why choose us? Highlighting the service.  
✅ **Menu Section** – Multiple dishes with name, price, and quick ordering option.  
✅ **Food Gallery** – Aesthetic food images to attract customers.  
✅ **Customer Reviews** – Testimonials for trust-building.  
✅ **Order Form** – Collects user name, contact, order details, and delivery address.  
✅ **Team Section** – Showcasing professional chefs.  
✅ **Global Locations** – Branches in multiple countries.  
✅ **Quick Links & Contact Info** – Easy navigation & customer support.  
✅ **Services Highlight** – Fast Delivery, Easy Payments, 24/7 Service.  

---

## 📸 Pages Overview  

- **🏠 Home** – Landing section with "Get Fresh Food in an Easy Way".  
- **ℹ️ About Us** – Why customers should choose this restaurant.  
- **📖 Menu** – Burgers, Pizza, Pasta, Lasagna, Drinks, Hot Dogs, Biryani, Chocolate, Ice Cream, Sandwiches & more.  
- **🖼️ Gallery** – Beautiful food presentation.  
- **💬 Reviews** – Customer testimonials with feedback.  
- **🛒 Order Page** – Place orders quickly with name, email, number, food item, and address.  
- **👨‍🍳 Team** – Meet the chefs behind the kitchen.  
- **📍 Location** – Sri Lanka, USA, India, Japan, Italy.
